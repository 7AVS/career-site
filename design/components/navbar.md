# Navbar Component

## Layout
- Sticky, appears on scroll (or always visible)
- Left: Initials in bold (e.g., "AC" for Andre Chen — or "AD" for Andre)
- Right: "Experience" | "Fit Check" | [Ask AI button]
- Ask AI button: teal background, rounded-full, white/dark text

## States
- **Default**: Transparent/dark background
- **Scrolled**: Semi-transparent with backdrop blur
- **Mobile**: Hamburger menu or simplified layout

## Behavior
- Clicking nav links smooth-scrolls to section
- "Ask AI" opens the chat modal

## From Reference
The Marcus Chen site shows:
- "MC" initials left
- "Experience" and "Fit Check" as text links
- "Ask AI" as a teal pill button
- Thin teal line at top of the nav bar
